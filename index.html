Abstract

Method

FOM-SAM3 retrieves a persistent FO identity, and FSAE converts the selected objects into focused policy conditions.

Two contributions, one focused policy

Persistent FO Memory. A few registered views create reusable identity tokens while SAM3 remains fully frozen.

Focused policy conditioning. FSAE preserves ordered target and manipulation-context appearance and geometry for either DP or ACT.

Experiments

REAL-ROBOT MANIPULATION

DemoFO skill→SimilarFO distractors→Switch FO memory→Reuse frozen policy

Generalization

Frozen-policy reuse. The same frozen policy operates on different registered SimilarFOs without retraining; only the FO Memory Tokens are switched.
